   # Privacy Policy

   Weekly Briefing is a personal automation tool built and used solely by
   its owner for their own private use. It is not distributed publicly.

   **Data accessed:** Google Calendar (read-only) and Gmail (send-only —
   it cannot read, delete, or modify any existing email) belonging to the
   single Google account that authorises it.

   **Data storage:** All calendar data is stored locally on a personal
   device controlled solely by the app's owner, and is never sold or
   shared with any third party. The only external service it contacts is
   Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com), a weather forecast API, which
   receives only a geographic coordinate — no personal or calendar data.
